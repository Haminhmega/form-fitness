Privacy & your data

Last updated October 14, 2026

Your workout videos stay on this device.

Form’s experimental movement analysis runs in your browser. Selected clips and analysis frames are not uploaded or saved to your account.

Account information

If you create a Form account, Form stores your sign-in identity, profile, optional body measurements, preferences, routines, and completed workout history so they can sync across devices. Active workouts remain on the device until completed.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in requests basic identity information: your Google account ID, email address, and display name. Form does not receive or store your Google password. You can link Google to an existing Form account from Profile.

Copies on your device

When you choose account sync, Form keeps an offline copy in this browser profile. Anyone using the same unlocked browser profile may be able to read it. Signing out clears that account’s Form copy on the current device.

Connected-device demos

Fitbit, WHOOP, and other device imports in this prototype use clearly labeled sample data. They do not connect to a real wearable account yet.

Delete your data

Use Profile → Delete my Form account to remove the synced profile, stats, routines, workout history, Google identity link, and active Form sessions. Other devices can retain an offline copy until they reconnect or sign out. Deleting Form data does not delete your Google or ChatGPT account.

Data use

Form does not sell account data. The prototype does not upload workout videos, use them for model training, or send them to an AI service.
