Privacy & your data

Last updated October 23, 2026

Your workout videos stay on this device.

Form’s experimental movement analysis runs in your browser. Selected or in-app recorded clips and analysis frames are not uploaded or saved to your account.

Filming in Form

The camera is requested only when you choose to film. Recording uses video only, with no microphone. Your countdown, preview and recorded take stay in this browser. The camera is released when you finish, cancel, leave the recorder or hide the app. Clips are temporary and are cleared when you leave the analysis screen; Form does not automatically save them to Photos.

Account information

If you create a Form account, Form stores your email address, display name, a salted password hash, profile, optional body measurements, preferences, routines, and completed workout history so they can sync across devices. Form does not store your plaintext password. Active workouts remain on the device until completed.

Email confirmation and recovery

Form sends confirmation and password-reset codes through Resend. Resend receives your destination email address and the email content. Codes expire after 10 minutes, have limited attempts, and are stored as keyed hashes. Changing your email requires your current password and verification of the new address. Resetting a password or changing an email revokes Form sessions. Email addresses and hashed request identifiers are used to limit abuse; expired records are cleaned up during subsequent requests.

Beta usage reporting

Basic usage reporting is on by default for signed-in beta accounts. You do not need to enable it in settings. An existing opt-out remains off. You can turn it off anytime in Profile → Help improve Form → Share basic usage activity. A non-blocking in-app notice explains this default. It records app opens and activity timestamps against a pseudonymous account identifier, not your email, videos, body measurements or exercise choices. Form uses these records to improve the product and create aggregate usage reports for potential investors. Only the verified owner account can access the aggregate dashboard. Records expire after 90 days and are removed when you opt out or delete your account. Reports include only participating signed-in accounts and are not a count of all users. Previously downloaded aggregate reports cannot be recalled.

Training rewards

When you use a synced profile, Form stores your reward time zone, weekly goals, earning dates, workout identifiers, points, credits, potion inventory and purchase references with your account. These records prevent repeated rewards and charges. Removing a workout does not remove rewards already recorded; deleting your Form account removes the rewards record. This browser may keep an account-specific purchase reference to retry an interrupted purchase. Credits have no cash value. Ad rewards are not connected and no advertising SDK was added.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in requests basic identity information: your Google account ID, email address, and display name. Form does not receive or store your Google password. You can link Google to an existing Form account from Profile.

Copies on your device

When you choose account sync, Form keeps an offline copy in this browser profile. Anyone using the same unlocked browser profile may be able to read it. Signing out clears that account’s Form copy on the current device.

Delete your data

Use Profile → Delete my Form account to remove the synced profile, stats, routines, workout history, reward records, usage events and consent, Form sign-in identity, linked Google identity, and active Form sessions. Other devices can retain an offline copy until they reconnect or sign out. Deleting Form data does not delete your Google or ChatGPT account.

Data use

Form does not sell account data. The prototype does not upload workout videos, use them for model training, or send them to an AI service.
